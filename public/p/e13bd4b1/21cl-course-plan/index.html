21st Century Lawyering Course Plan, Spring 2027

Ohio State Moritz College of Law · Rebecca Fordon Working draft, Spring 2027

21st Century Lawyering is an upper-level JD course on the technology lawyers actually use, with generative AI at the center. Students learn how the tools work, use them on realistic legal tasks, build small tools of their own, and practice checking what the tools produce.

I have taught it every spring since 2024. Every session meets in person and most include hands-on work with the tools.

Course goalHelp future lawyers put technology, and AI in particular, to work in real legal practice, build things with it, and develop the judgment to know when it works, when it does not, and how to tell the difference.
Students
Upper-level JD. Recent classes split across big firms, small and mid-size firms, and government or public interest work.

How students are assessed

Students are graded on a team tool-evaluation presentation, where each team tests one legal task across several AI tools and makes a recommendation to their peers; a final project, either a paper or a tool they build, developed from proposal through outline to final; a short set of document-skills modules; and in-class work that starts with a reflection on the day's reading.
