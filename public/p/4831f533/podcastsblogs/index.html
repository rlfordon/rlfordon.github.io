Podcasts & Current Awareness

Podcasts, newsletters, and other ways to keep up with law, chosen for Moritz students and faculty. Grouped by course subject, with law school, the profession, and legal news beside them.

How to use this guide

Finding more

This guide collects podcasts, newsletters, and other current-awareness tools relevant to your studies and interests in law. Each entry has a short description and a link. Group the list by course subject, kind, or how you can get access, or search it; the Moritz picks are shows produced by our own faculty, centers, and programs.

What are legal newsletters?

Legal newsletters are regular updates that provide legal professionals with the latest news, analysis, and commentary on legal developments and industry trends, including new case law and legislation. These newsletters may cover a broad range of topics, such as litigation, regulatory changes, and emerging areas of law. Law students might use newsletters to:

  • Stay up-to-date with the latest legal developments and industry trends.
  • Get insights into different areas of law and potential career paths.
  • Develop a deeper understanding of legal issues and concepts.
  • Gain exposure to different legal writing styles and formats.
  • Identify potential research topics or areas of interest for further study.

To find newsletters, review the list, or ask a librarian: if you are having trouble finding titles in your practice area, contact a law librarian through the library's Ask a Librarian page. Another way to find newsletters is a web search for your practice area along with "law newsletter libguide", which leads to research guides created by librarians with recommendations for specific newsletters.

Access to subscription newsletters

Many vendors provide newsletters, and the right one for you will depend on your practice area and which vendors you can access. Moritz Law Library provides access to Bloomberg Law, Law360 (through Lexis+), Vital Law, Practical Law, Westlaw, Checkpoint Edge, and ALM's Law Journal Newsletters; each entry says how to log in. Access is limited to OSU faculty, students, and staff.
